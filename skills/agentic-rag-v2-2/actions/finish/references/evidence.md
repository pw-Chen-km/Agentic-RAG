# FINISH evidence rules

Eligible citations are complete visible Sentences and fully read Chunks selected
by Stage 1. Entities, Sentence previews, and unread Chunks cannot support a
FINISH action.

For a bridge question, cite both the subject-to-bridge fact and the
bridge-to-answer fact. For a comparison, cite the value for every compared
subject. Preserve supported qualifiers and compound wording rather than
shortening the answer.

Do not treat a topic match as factual support. Each answer claim must be stated
or directly entailed by at least one citation.
